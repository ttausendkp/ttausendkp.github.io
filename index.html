I am a first year mathematics PhD-student at the University of Copenhagen, in the group of Prof. Dr. Ryomei Iwasa. My work is in motivic homotopy theory, more specifically in the subfield of Non $\mathbb{A}^1$-invariant motivic homotopy theory and I am especially interested in the . Before this I completed my Master's at the Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz, under the supervision of Prof. Dr. Tom Bachmann and Prof. Dr. Marc Hoyois, on the topic of A motivic Note on a proof by Quillen.
